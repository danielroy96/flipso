v1.1:
Rail tickets now name the office that sold them: online, telesales, app and travel-centre sales, and station ticket machines as, for example, "High Wycombe (TVM)", where they used to read "Station NNNN". A seller the table cannot name reads "Retailer NNNN". Transport for London is named as an operator on GWR Touch taps at Paddington. Scanning has its own sound, light and vibration: yellow while the reader waits, a rising run with green on a good read, and three red buzzes on a failed one.

v1.0:
First release in the Flipper Apps Catalog. Reads ITSO smartcards (CMD7 DESFire, CMD2), Glasgow Subway paper tickets (CMD4) and ITSO shells on NTAG and Ultralight EV1 tags (CMD9, CMD10). Shows tickets, pay as you go, cardholder ID and entitlements, and recent journeys, with station, bus stop and operator names. Cards can be saved to the SD card and reopened later.
