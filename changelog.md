v1.0:
First release in the Flipper Apps Catalog. Reads ITSO smartcards (CMD7 DESFire, CMD2), Glasgow Subway paper tickets (CMD4) and ITSO shells on NTAG and Ultralight EV1 tags (CMD9, CMD10). Shows tickets, pay as you go, cardholder ID and entitlements, and recent journeys, with station, bus stop and operator names. Cards can be saved to the SD card and reopened later.
